Drywall Repair vs Drywall Replacement: What Homeowners Should Know

Drywall damage can happen from moisture, accidental impact, settling, remodeling work, or plumbing leaks. Small cracks and holes are often repairable, while larger damage may require section replacement.

Professional drywall repair can restore smooth wall surfaces before painting or texture application. In cases involving water damage or mold exposure, replacement may be the safer long-term option.

Homeowners should inspect wall condition, moisture sources, and texture matching needs before deciding between repair and replacement. A remodeling contractor can help determine the best approach.
