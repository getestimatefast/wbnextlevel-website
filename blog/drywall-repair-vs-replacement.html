Drywall repair vs replacement: what homeowners should know.

Some drywall damage can be repaired cleanly, while larger or moisture-damaged areas may require replacement.

When drywall repair makes sense

Small holes, dents, cracks, corner damage, and surface imperfections are often repairable.

When replacement may be needed

Major water damage, mold concerns, repeated repairs, or larger structural problems may require drywall replacement.

Preparing for painting or remodeling

Drywall condition affects the final appearance of paint and remodeling finishes. Clean preparation matters.
