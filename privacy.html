Privacy Policy

Last updated May 15, 2026

This page explains how WBNextlevel handles information submitted through estimate forms, text messages, email, and website contact links.

Information we collect

When you request an estimate, WBNextlevel may collect your name, phone number, email address, city, preferred contact method, service needed, project description, and photos or details you choose to send by text or email.

How we use your information

We use this information to respond to your remodeling or home improvement request, discuss your project, schedule next steps, provide estimate information, and communicate about services you requested.

Text messages and email

If you text WBNextlevel or submit your phone number, we may reply by text about your project. Standard message and data rates may apply. You can ask us to stop texting at any time.

Sharing information

WBNextlevel does not sell personal information. Information may be processed through tools used to receive form submissions, email, text messages, website hosting, or analytics.

Contact

For privacy questions, email wbnextlevel@gmail.com or text 813-591-7560.
